# Top Business Process Management (BPM) Ecosystem


**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Business Process Management, Workflow Automation, Process Orchestration, Case Management, Decision Automation & Process Intelligence*  
**Last updated: August 2026**


This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Business Process Management (BPM)**. These tools help organizations model, automate, execute, monitor, optimize, and govern business processes involving people, applications, data, documents, decisions, and system integrations.


**Examples** include ProcessMaker, Appian, Pega, Nintex, Kissflow, Pipefy, Bonita, Bizagi, IBM Business Automation Workflow, and Creatio.


**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with open-source BPM suites, workflow engines, case-management platforms, business-rule engines, process-modeling tools, orchestration frameworks, low-code platforms, and workflow infrastructure. Particular emphasis is placed on **Camunda, Flowable, jBPM, ProcessMaker, Bonita, Joget, Activiti, Kogito, Operaton, Temporal, and other open-source projects** that can be self-hosted and extended.


BPM is a broad category. Some projects below are complete BPM platforms, while others are **BPMN engines, workflow runtimes, case-management engines, rules engines, or orchestration frameworks** that can be used as the foundation of a custom BPM platform. Flowable, for example, provides open-source BPMN, CMMN, and DMN engines under Apache 2.0, while jBPM is an open-source toolkit for building applications that automate business processes and decisions. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms


- **[ProcessMaker](https://www.processmaker.com/)**  
  Business process management and workflow-automation platform supporting process modeling, forms, document workflows, approvals, integrations, task management, and process automation. ProcessMaker also has an open-source BPM codebase. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}


- **[Appian](https://www.appian.com/)**  
  Enterprise low-code automation platform combining process orchestration, workflow automation, case management, data management, AI, RPA, and application development.


- **[Pega](https://www.pega.com/)**  
  Enterprise process-automation and case-management platform focused on customer service, operations, decisioning, workflow automation, and complex enterprise processes.


- **[Nintex](https://www.nintex.com/)**  
  Process-management and workflow-automation platform providing process mapping, workflow automation, document generation, forms, process intelligence, and automation across enterprise applications.


- **[Kissflow](https://kissflow.com/)**  
  Low-code/no-code workflow and process-automation platform designed for business teams to build applications, approvals, workflows, forms, and operational processes.


- **[Pipefy](https://www.pipefy.com/)**  
  No-code process-management and workflow-automation platform commonly used for procurement, finance, HR, service operations, and other structured business processes.


- **[Bonita](https://www.bonitasoft.com/)**  
  BPM and application-development platform based around process modeling, workflow automation, application development, connectors, and process execution.


- **[Bizagi](https://www.bizagi.com/)**  
  Business process-management platform centered around BPMN modeling, process automation, digital applications, workflow orchestration, and process optimization.


- **[IBM Business Automation Workflow](https://www.ibm.com/products/business-automation-workflow)**  
  Enterprise workflow and case-management platform for orchestrating human tasks, business processes, content, decisions, and applications.


- **[Creatio](https://www.creatio.com/)**  
  No-code platform combining business process management, CRM, workflow automation, application development, and composable business applications.


- **[Camunda](https://camunda.com/)**  
  Process orchestration platform centered on BPMN-based process execution, workflow orchestration, decision automation, human tasks, and integration of distributed systems.


- **[SAP Signavio](https://www.sap.com/products/business-transformation-management.html)**
